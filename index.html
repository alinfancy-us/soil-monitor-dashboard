What's new

    🎯 Sensor Calibration

    Two-point calibration reduces measurement errors and improves soil moisture accuracy.Place the probe in the matching condition, click the button, and the device will recalibrate using a new reading.

    Connect device to enable calibration

    🌡️ Temperature Offset

    Fine-tune the reported temperature with an adjustable offset from -18 °F to +18 °F (-10 ℃ to +10 ℃). Saved on device (persists across reboots). Use the − / + buttons for precise 0.1 steps, or type an exact value in the box.

    ℃
    -10.0 ℃ ~ +10.0 ℃

    🏷️ Device Name

    A display name saved on the device and shown in this dashboard. Up to 10 ASCII characters. It does not change the device's default Bluetooth broadcast name, So nothing needs to be re-paired in Home Assistant.

    0 / 10 characters

    ⚠️ Reset

    Clears history, calibration, temperature offset, and device name on the device, then reboots it. This page's locally cached data for the device is also cleared. The connection will drop.

    SoilPulse delivers real-time root-zone insights straight to your device. Insert into soil, tap, and connect — get instant soil insights in seconds.

    • Root-Zone Accuracy — moisture and temperature right where roots drink.
    • Instant Local Access — open directly in browser; 100% private with no cloud subscription.
    • Smart Home Ready — seamless Home Assistant integration via Bluetooth.

    Quick Setup

    1. Install batteries — Open cover, insert 2× AAA (+/−), seal tightly.
    2. Insert into soil — Insert probe to the OPTIMAL DEPTH line (≈ 2.4 in). Pack soil around probe.
    3. Wake sensor — Tap the top touch pad to activate the device.

    Touch to Wake & Connect

    Connect — Home Assistant

    Powered by BTHome — requires Bluetooth enabled in Home Assistant.

    1. Open HA — Navigate to Settings → Devices & Services.
    2. Wake sensor — Tap touch pad to make it discoverable.
    3. Add device — Click Add on the discovered SoilPulse card.
    ⚡ Power Saver Design: Sensor broadcasts every 10 min. Brief “Unavailable” status between updates in HA is normal.

    Troubleshooting

    • Can’t connect? Wake sensor by tapping touch pad, grant Bluetooth permission, then tap Connect device.
    • No new data? Click Refresh after connecting — updates in a few seconds.
    • Odd readings? Insert probe fully to the OPTIMAL DEPTH line; avoid air gaps in soil.
    • Unavailable in HA? Normal — sensor broadcasts every 10 min. Data arrives automatically.

    Battery Safety

    Use 2× AAA batteries. Do not mix old and new batteries, or different brands. Remove batteries if the device is stored for an extended period to prevent leakage.
