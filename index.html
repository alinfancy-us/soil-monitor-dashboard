What's new

    🎯 Sensor Calibration

    Two-point calibration reduces measurement errors and improves soil moisture accuracy.Place the probe in the matching condition, click the button, and the device will recalibrate using a new reading.

    Connect device to enable calibration

    🌡️ Temperature Offset

    Fine-tune the reported temperature with an adjustable offset from -18 °F to +18 °F (-10 ℃ to +10 ℃). Saved on device (persists across reboots). Use the − / + buttons for precise 0.1 steps, or type an exact value in the box.

    -10.0 ℃ ~ +10.0 ℃

    🏷️ Device Name

    A display name saved on the device and shown in this dashboard. Up to 10 ASCII characters. It does not change the device's default Bluetooth broadcast name, So nothing needs to be re-paired in Home Assistant.

    0 / 10 characters

    ⚠️ Reset

    Clears history, calibration, temperature offset, and device name on the device, then reboots it. This page's locally cached data for the device is also cleared. The connection will drop.

    Introduction

    SoilPulse monitors your soil — moisture, temperature and battery. Just plant, connect, and check on the spot.

    • Live soil metrics — true root-zone moisture & temperature (not surface estimates), plus battery status.
    • No app required — open directly in the Bluetooth-enabled browser.
    • Seamless Home Assistant integration, no gateway needed.

    Meet Your Sensor

    Setup

    1. Insert 2× AAA batteries.
    2. Plant the probe to the OPTIMAL DEPTH line (≈ 2.4 in).
    3. Tap the touch pad to wake it.
    4. Choose your setup method — follow the guides below to connect via Web Dashboard or Home Assistant.

    Connect — Web Dashboard

    Requires a Bluetooth-enabled phone or PC with Bluetooth turned on.

    Scan to open Dashboard

    • Opens in your browser instantly

    • Built-in interactive setup guide

    Can't find it? Tap touch pad to wake device and allow Bluetooth permissions.
    • Android / PC: Use Chrome.
    • iPhone / iPad: Download & use Bluefy app (iOS Safari/Chrome does not support Web Bluetooth).

    Connect — Home Assistant

    Uses BTHome— Requires Bluetooth enabled in Home Assistant.

    1. Settings → Devices & Services → Add device.
    2. Tap the sensor's touch pad to wake it.
    3. HA adds temperature, moisture & battery.

    Using the Web Dashboard

    • Data Tab — live temperature, moisture & battery; history with last 5 readings and 7-day average charts.
    • Refresh & Clear
      • Refresh: triggers an immediate measurement.
      • Clear Data: clears local & history data.
    • Setting Tab
      • Calibration & Temperature: Dry/Wet moisture calibration & temperature offset adjustments.
      • Device Management: Custom device renaming & factory reset.
      • Firmware Updates: Displays a prompt when new updates are available.

    Troubleshooting

    • Can't connect? Tap the touch pad to wake it, allow Bluetooth permissions, then click Connect.
    • No new data? Connect and click Refresh — new data should arrive within a few seconds.
    • Odd moisture? Insert the probe to the OPTIMAL DEPTH line without leaving loose gaps in soil.
    • Shows unavailable in Home Assistant? This is normal. The sensor updates every 10 minutes to save power — new data will still arrive automatically.
