    Sensor calibration

    Uses the device's live moisture reading. Place the probe first, tap the matching calibration button below, and wait a few minutes to see the updated calibrated moisture.

    Temperature offset

    Correct the soil temperature reading by up to ±10 ℃. The offset is stored on the device in memory and cleared after a factory reset or battery replacement.

    Reset

    Welcome to SoilPulse 👋

    SoilPulse is a Bluetooth soil sensor that measures soil temperature, moisture and battery level. This quick guide helps you get started in a few minutes.

    📄 Download introduction (PDF)

    A printable quick-start guide — opens or saves as a PDF.

    Getting started

    1

    Tap “Connect device”

    Your browser opens a Bluetooth device list. Pick the SoilPulse detector closest to you.

    2

    Watch live readings

    Once connected, the Data tab shows temperature, moisture and battery — updated in real time.

    3

    Review trends

    Open the Data tab to browse every measurement and see 5-record or 7-day average charts.

    Calibration

    In the Setting tab, place the probe fully dry in open air and tap Dry calibration, then submerge the probe in water and tap Wet calibration. This improves moisture accuracy for the soil you use.

    Understanding readings

    • 🌡️ Temperature — soil temperature, shown in °F by default (switch to °C). Most plants thrive between 59–86 °F (15–30 ℃).
    • 💧 Moisture — relative soil moisture as a percentage. Aim for the sweet spot of your plant.
    • 🔋 Battery — remaining charge. Recharge before it gets too low.

    Firmware update

    When a newer firmware is available, the Setting tab shows an update hint and a red dot. Open it, read “What's new”, then tap Update now to upgrade over the air — the device reboots automatically after a successful update.

    Troubleshooting

    • Bluetooth unavailable? Use Google Chrome on desktop or the Bluefy app on iOS. On Android WeChat, tap the menu and choose “Open in Browser”.
    • Not connecting? Make sure the sensor is powered on and close to your phone or computer, then tap Refresh.
    • Odd data? Use Clear data to remove cached readings for the current device, then reconnect.
